![Sex and the City Cover](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/sex-and-the-city-cover.avif)

Lately I’ve been obsessed with re-watching shows, especially if they involve living a fabulous life in New York. I just finished Gossip Girl (_not bad_) but you can’t beat the classic: Sex and the City. Their beauty is intoxicating. They don’t clash with each other. Their personalities and lifestyles make you wish you had a little bit of each of them in you.

I want to be a writer like Carrie.  
Intimidatingly successful like Miranda.  
Old-money and timeless like Charlotte.  
And, of course, wild and untamed like Samantha.

The men in their lives are no joke either. There may be strong opinions about Carrie’s love for Mr. Big but deep down, I yearn for a love that burns so brightly, my soul would never be the same.

These women showed us that love and men come in all different flavors.  
So which one’s yours?

**1\. Eros – Romantic / Passionate Love**

_Eros is the kind of love that consumes you, a passionate, magnetic force that makes your heart race and your world shrink to one person. It’s the spark that feels like fate, the kind that keeps you awake at night replaying every word, every glance. Rooted in desire and attraction, Eros is intoxicating and raw, but also fleeting if it isn’t nurtured into something deeper. It teaches you what it means to crave, to risk, and to lose yourself in another. A reminder that while passion can ignite love, it alone cannot sustain it._

## Carrie & Mr. Big

![Carrie and Mr. Big](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/carrie-and-mr-big.jpg)

Their relationship is pure Eros: intense, magnetic, and impossible to ignore. Carrie and Big are drawn to each other through passion and chemistry that defy logic, but their love burns too hot to last peacefully. They’re proof that sometimes, attraction alone isn’t enough to sustain something real.

**2\. Philia – Deep Friendship or Affectionate Love**

_Philia is the love that feels like safety, a steady warmth rather than a burning flame. It’s the deep affection shared between friends, built on trust, understanding, and mutual respect. Unlike Eros, it isn’t driven by desire but by genuine care and connection. Philia is the kind of love that grows slowly through shared laughter, late-night talks, and showing up for each other when it matters most. It teaches you that love doesn’t always need grand gestures or passion to be real; sometimes, it’s simply knowing that someone sees you, accepts you, and stays._

## Carrie & Aidan

![Carrie and Aidan](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/carrie-and-aidan.png)

Aidan represents comfort and genuine care, the kind of love that feels safe. With him, Carrie experiences a partnership built on trust and friendship rather than chaos. He shows her a softer kind of love, one that doesn’t demand, just is.

**3\. Storge – Familial / Familiar Love**

_Storge is the love that feels like home, quiet, constant, and often taken for granted until it’s gone. It’s the natural affection that forms through familiarity: the bond between parent and child, siblings, or even lifelong companions who’ve grown into family. Unlike the thrill of Eros or the choice of Pragma, Storge is effortless, it exists simply because it does. It teaches you the meaning of unconditional care, patience, and belonging, even when words fall short. This kind of love reminds you that some connections aren’t chosen; they’re woven into the fabric of who you are._

## Miranda & Steve

![Miranda and Steve](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/miranda-and-steve.png)

Miranda and Steve’s love grows out of familiarity, routine, and shared life. They bicker, compromise, and co-parent, but their love endures because it’s built on loyalty and a sense of “home.” It’s not glamorous, it’s real.

**4\. Agape – Selfless / Unconditional Love**

_Agape is the purest form of love: selfless, unconditional, and boundless. It’s the kind of love that gives without expecting anything in return, rooted in compassion and understanding. Agape extends beyond romance or friendship; it’s found in the quiet ways you forgive, the patience you offer, and the empathy you show even to those who’ve hurt you. It’s the love that asks nothing but to see others at peace. This is the kind of love that transforms, when you’ve been through heartbreak, loss, and growth, Agape becomes the gentle strength that guides you to love without fear and to find meaning in kindness itself._

## Samantha & Smith

![Samantha and Smith](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/samantha-and-smith.png)

Smith’s love for Samantha is selfless. He stands by her through illness, fame, and fear, never asking for more than she can give. Their relationship shows that love at its highest form is not about possession but unconditional care.

**5\. Philautia – Self-Love**

_Philautia is the love that begins and ends with you, the quiet acceptance of your flaws, your worth, and your becoming. It’s not vanity or selfishness, but the understanding that you cannot pour from an empty cup. Philautia grows when you stop seeking validation from others and start honoring your own needs, boundaries, and dreams. It’s forgiving yourself for who you were, celebrating who you are, and believing in who you’re still becoming. This kind of love teaches you that every heartbreak, every failure, every triumph has been guiding you back to the same place, yourself._

## Samantha & Richard

![Samantha and Richard](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/samantha-and-richard.png)

Her relationship with Richard shatters her illusions and becomes the turning point in learning to love herself. Through betrayal and heartbreak, Samantha realizes that self-respect and self-love must come before any man.

**6\. Ludus – Playful / Flirtatious Love**

_Ludus is the love that laughs: light, playful, and intoxicating in its simplicity. It’s the teasing glances, the inside jokes, the effortless fun of being with someone who makes you feel alive without the weight of forever. Ludus thrives on spontaneity and flirtation; it’s love that dances rather than settles. While it may not always lead to deep commitment, it reminds you that love can be joyful and free, that not every connection needs to be heavy to be meaningful. This kind of love teaches you to enjoy the moment, to flirt with life itself and rediscover the beauty of simply feeling good._

## Samantha & Her Flings

![Samantha Jones](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/samantha-jones-and-her-flings.png)

Samantha embodies Ludus effortlessly. Her flirtations are fun, fiery, and fleeting, connections meant to be enjoyed, not tamed. She reminds us that not every love story has to end with forever; some are meant to be lived in the moment.

**7\. Pragma – Enduring / Practical Love**

_Pragma is the love that endures: steady, mature, and grounded in choice rather than impulse. It’s the kind of love that grows over time, built through patience, trust, and shared effort. Unlike Eros, which burns brightly at the start, Pragma is the slow, lasting flame that keeps you warm through seasons of change. It’s choosing the same person even on the hard days, showing up when the excitement fades, and finding comfort in the ordinary. This love teaches you that real commitment isn’t about perfection or constant passion, it’s about partnership, understanding, and the quiet decision to stay and keep growing together._

## Charlotte & Harry

![Charlotte and Harry](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/charlotte-and-harry.png)

Charlotte and Harry prove that lasting love is a choice. It’s built on patience, compromise, and the courage to stay even when the fantasy fades. They show that real love isn’t perfect: it’s practical, kind, and enduring.

**8\. Mania – Obsessive / Possessive Love**

_Mania is the love that loses balance: intense, consuming, and often born from fear rather than connection. It’s the kind of love that clings too tightly, where passion turns into obsession and affection becomes dependence. Mania thrives on uncertainty, feeding off the highs and lows of attention, constantly needing reassurance to feel secure. It can make you mistake chaos for intensity and control for care. This kind of love teaches you the hardest lesson: that love without trust, space, or self-worth can’t survive. It’s a reminder that real love should make you feel free, not trapped inside someone else’s approval._

## Carrie & Aleksandr Petrovsky

![Carrie and Aleksandr Petrovsky](/images/sex-and-the-city-types-of-love/carrie-and-aleks.png)

Their relationship begins as a dazzling whirlwind but soon unravels under imbalance. Carrie gives too much, losing herself in his world, while Aleksandr remains distant. It’s a love that consumes rather than nurtures, beautiful but suffocating.

**The Bottom Line**

We all know how complicated love can be. It doesn’t belong to just one category... it shifts, grows, and transforms us along the way. Every connection teaches us something, even the ones that break us open. What matters most is recognizing the kind of love you’re in and asking yourself if it’s enough, or if it’s time for it to become something more.

_Maybe the question isn’t about which is your favorite flavor,_  
_but rather which ones go well together._
